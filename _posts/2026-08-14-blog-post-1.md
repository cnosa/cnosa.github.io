I enjoy writing. For me, writing is a way to organize ideas, explore questions, and share what I'm learning or thinking. That's why it feels natural to do it on my own website, a personal space where I can gather and preserve my writing without relying on other platforms.

In these blog posts, I'll publish short articles on topics that interest me, especially those related to mathematics, science, research, and other ideas I find fascinating. I don't necessarily intend to follow a single theme; the goal is to gradually build a collection of texts that reflects my interests and my way of thinking.
